About the Studio

We are SZY Innovation Studio.
Founded in 2024 by several middle and elementary school students, we are a non-profit technology studio.
We focus on coding and developing interesting and practical projects to serve the online community. The studio is also active in public welfare projects, using technology to empower society.
We look forward to collaborating with like-minded friends from all fields.
